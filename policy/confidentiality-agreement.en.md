# Mutual Confidentiality Agreement (English version)

> **Reference translation only.** The Spanish text in [confidentiality-agreement.md](confidentiality-agreement.md) is the version to review and sign; it prevails over this translation. Draft for legal review: not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Bracketed fields are completed for each client.

In [city], on [date], the following parties appear:

**[DIRECTIO LEGAL NAME]**, [company type], domiciled at [address], represented by [name], as [title], hereinafter **"Directio"**; and

**[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]**, [company type], domiciled at [address], represented by [name], as [title], hereinafter **"the Client"**.

Directio and the Client, together "the Parties" and each "a Party", agree as follows:

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate a possible collaboration for the diagnosis and implementation of artificial intelligence solutions in the Client's processes (the "Purpose"). For this they may exchange Confidential Information. This agreement governs its use and protection.

## 2. Confidential Information

All information, in any format, that one Party (the "Disclosing Party") delivers or makes known to the other (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Purpose, including: operational, financial, commercial and cost data; customer, supplier and price lists; processes, designs, drawings and product specifications; personal data of employees, customers or third parties; and Directio's methodologies, templates and tools.

Confidential Information does not include information the Receiving Party shows: (a) was public when received or became public through no fault of its own; (b) it already lawfully knew before receiving it; (c) it lawfully received from a third party with no confidentiality obligation; or (d) it developed independently without using the Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

1. Use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose.
2. Not disclose it to third parties without the Disclosing Party's prior written authorization, except to its employees, partners and advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.
3. Protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and never less than reasonable care.
4. Notify the Disclosing Party within 72 hours of confirming any unauthorized access, use or disclosure, stating the measures taken.

## 4. Personal data and use of artificial intelligence

Regarding personal data and the Client's information, Directio further agrees to:

1. Process personal data only for the Purpose and according to the Client's instructions.
2. Request and process only the data strictly necessary.
3. Pseudonymize or remove personal identifiers before processing the information with artificial intelligence tools, unless the Client authorizes otherwise in writing.
4. Use only artificial intelligence tools whose terms exclude using the information to train models, and provide the Client, on request, the list of tools and providers with access to its information.
5. Not use the Client's information for the benefit of other clients or to develop third-party products.

## 5. Ownership

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. This agreement grants no license or right to it, except use for the Purpose. Directio's pre-existing methodologies, templates and tools remain Directio's.

## 6. Return and deletion

When the Purpose ends, or earlier if the Disclosing Party requests it in writing, the Receiving Party will return or delete the Confidential Information within 30 days and confirm it in writing. It may keep a copy only where the law requires, subject to this agreement.

## 7. Compelled disclosure

If a competent authority requires disclosure of Confidential Information, the Receiving Party will promptly notify the Disclosing Party (where the law allows) and disclose only what is strictly required.

## 8. Term

This agreement takes effect on signature and lasts [two (2)] years. Confidentiality obligations survive [three (3)] years after termination, and indefinitely for personal data and trade secrets.

## 9. No obligation to contract

This agreement does not oblige either Party to enter into further contracts.

## 10. Breach

The Party in breach is liable for the damages it causes, without prejudice to any other legal action available.

## 11. Governing law and disputes

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Guatemala. The Parties will try to resolve any dispute in good faith; failing that within [30] days, they submit to [the competent courts of Guatemala City / arbitration under the rules of (arbitration center)].

## 12. General provisions

Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. If a clause is held invalid, the others remain valid.

Having read the above, the Parties accept it and sign in two counterparts.
