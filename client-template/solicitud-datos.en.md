# Data request: <client>

Sent to the client when the first meeting closes, with the NDA signed or out for signature. We ask for little and for specific things: just enough to measure one process.

## 1. Client-side AI account (ask first)

We work inside the client's licenses from day one, so the data and what we build stay with them.

- [ ] Which business AI plan do you have? (ChatGPT Business or Enterprise, Claude Team or Enterprise, Gemini in Workspace, Microsoft Copilot)
- [ ] A user for Directio on that plan, which you can revoke at any time.
- [ ] Confirm that training on your data is off (business plans ship with it off; we check it together).
- [ ] If we will use the API: a project key with a monthly spending cap that you set.
- [ ] If you have no business plan: we note it and use a synthetic scenario until you have one. We never use free or personal accounts with your data.

## 2. Real examples of the process

- [ ] Between 5 and 20 recent, complete cases of the chosen process, end to end (the request that came in, what was produced, what was sent).
- [ ] Two or three cases that went wrong or took very long, if you have them.
- [ ] The template or format you use today for the output.
- [ ] Catalogs, price lists or tables the person looks up while working.

If they contain your customers' personal data, we ask for a version with names, phone numbers and ID numbers redacted, unless the process needs them.

## 3. Baseline

- [ ] How long does a case take today, from when it comes in until it goes out? (A range is fine; we measure it together in the first week.)
- [ ] How many cases per week or per month?
- [ ] Who does them and how many people are there?
- [ ] How do you know today whether a case came out right?

## 4. People

- [ ] The person who does the work, 45 minutes to watch them handle a real case.
- [ ] The person who reviews or approves it.
- [ ] A possible internal champion: someone curious and respected by their team.

## What we do not ask for

Access to core systems (ERP, accounting, production databases), passwords, or data the chosen process does not use.
