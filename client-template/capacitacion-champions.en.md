# Training and AI Companion Guide: <client>

Proposed route after the [area](diagnostico-area.en.md) and [champion](diagnostico-champion.en.md) diagnostics and [pilot](propuesta-piloto.en.md) preparation. Participants, frequency and dates: <agreed or pending>. Six 75-minute sessions total 7 hours and 30 minutes of training; diagnostic effort is agreed and recorded separately.

Each person uses the same process throughout. Between sessions they perform a bounded practice estimated at 15 to 30 minutes; choose a smaller step if the process takes longer. The facilitator reviews the output and proposes the next step based on what the person can do.

## Sessions

Start from the diagnostic's [visual map](mapa-proceso.en.md). In session 3 update the as is view with observations; in session 4 adjust the AI proposal and its human review. Keep IDs, sources, [C]/[I]/[P] marks and versions consistent across figures and profiles. Observing a trial or approving a proposal does not automatically confirm the entire current process.

| Axis and session | Objective | Exercise and deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations 1. Possibilities and limits | Recognize AI support, errors and human decisions. | Use the task selected in the diagnostic, compare instructions and detect a prepared error. Include ten minutes of warning signs: never paste passwords or card data, distrust links asking you to sign in, do not install extensions or connectors without asking, and who to tell if something leaked. Deliver review controls. |
| Foundations 2. A repeatable task | Give instructions and verify against the source. | Perform one step with permitted data; include missing inputs and contradictions. Deliver a reviewed template and corrections. |
| Ideal process 3. Validate the map | Compare the diagnostic with the first trials. | Complete exceptions, rework, dependencies and measurement. Deliver an updated map and reviewed baseline or plan. |
| Ideal process 4. Design the improvement | Improve a delivery while preserving controls. | Evaluate removing, simplifying or assisting steps. Deliver a proposed flow and adjusted pilot profile. |
| Agents 5. Coordinate across areas | Define a candidate agent and its limits. | Compare a template, automation and agent. Simulate inputs, outputs, rejection and escalation with a counterpart or by representing one. Apply the two-of-three rule: the agent may not simultaneously read untrusted content, hold private data and send something out. Use only connectors on the approved list. Deliver a design profile, the level and route of each step, and pending agreements. |
| Agents 6. Repeat and decide | Lead a repeat run and explain whether to proceed. | Run another normal case and an exception case. Simulate the agent design without connections or autonomous actions. Record support and recommend continuing, adjusting or stopping. |

If the person cannot imagine uses, start with what they must deliver and where they wait or repeat work; show options for those steps. Address skepticism by agreeing beforehand on a useful result and an error that would invalidate it. The candidate agent identifies its owner, sources, permitted actions, approvals, prohibitions, exceptions and recipient. Implementation needs technical evaluation and client authorization; training does not grant it.

## Personal guide between sessions

Use the start-here page in [adopcion.md](adopcion.en.md). Begin with the diagnostic's selected task and show only one next step. Each template includes:

1. **Title:** task in the person's words.
2. **When:** situation in which to use it.
3. **Data:** class, authorization and what to remove; fictitious if sufficient permission is missing.
4. **Text to paste:** full instruction with objective, inputs, output, limits and fields [to complete].
5. **It is right if:** one to four controls the person checks.
6. **Minutes before:** only the task's measured baseline; blank if unavailable.

If a separate panel is used, the proposed scope stores only name and role, data-free templates, usage outcome (yes, partly, no), minutes and date. Diagnostics, maps, files, conversations, outputs and detailed evaluations stay in the client's authorized environment. Present the panel and its access conditions for client approval before use. This template does not implement a panel or connect tools.

## Evaluation and continuity

Record whether the person explains the process, verifies outputs, leads a repeat run and defines a delivery to another area: can do it, needs support or has a pending condition, with evidence in the authorized environment. Separate learning from pilot results. Compare times only with a measured baseline and equivalent volume and scope, including review and correction; record setup and support separately. Do not claim operational savings from fictitious exercises.
