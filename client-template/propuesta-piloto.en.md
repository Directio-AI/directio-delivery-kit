## Autonomy level

Tick one. In phase 1 it is almost always 1 or 2.

- [ ] **1. Assists:** the AI prepares a draft; the person reviews, corrects and sends.
- [ ] **2. Reviews:** the AI does the work and flags doubts; the person approves case by case.
- [ ] **3. Acts with sampling:** the AI executes and the person reviews a sample. Only with measured real data and the client's written agreement.
