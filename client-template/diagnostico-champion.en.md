## Preparing the pilot and guide

Complete [propuesta-piloto.md](propuesta-piloto.en.md) with a bounded step, expected output, reviewer, limits and measurement. Prepare a fictitious scenario with normal cases, exceptions and an answer key, following the Delivery Kit's `escenario-sintetico.md` guide. Identify input and output agreements with other areas and initiatives that could be reused.

- [ ] Measured baseline or a plan to measure with comparable volume and scope; owners for pending items.
