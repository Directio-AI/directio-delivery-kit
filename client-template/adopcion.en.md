# Adoption: <client>

AI tools are powerful but overwhelming. Our job is to make them simple for the client's people: one assistant per role, one task at a time, in the tool they already have.

## Before training anyone

- [ ] Current [area diagnostic](diagnostico-area.en.md) and reviewed [champion diagnostic](diagnostico-champion.en.md): initial map, limits, reviewer, pilot and measurement plan. Reuse the area's current context for a new champion.
- [ ] Visible comparative maps per [mapa-proceso.md](mapa-proceso.en.md): as is, selected intervention and human review. Current figure and editable source, with [C]/[I]/[P] and visible pending items; the first task matches the pilot's intervention ID.
- [ ] The owner or management states clearly, in front of the team, what AI is for. Before that, in private, we ask whether the real aim is to cut headcount: if it is, we know before asking anyone for their trust.
- [ ] An internal champion chosen: curious, respected, with protected time agreed for sessions and practice.
- [ ] One assistant configured per role in the client's account, with instructions and authorized inputs. First task and template ready from the diagnostic; use fictitious data if sufficient permission for real data is missing.

For the process champion route use [capacitacion-champions.md](capacitacion-champions.en.md): six 75-minute sessions and the Companion Guide. The brief script below is an alternative start; if using that route, adapt its activities to session 1 instead of adding another required session.

## First session script (60 minutes, per role, at most 8 people)

| Minute | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | The sponsor says what this is for and what it is not for. |
| 5–15 | Each person confirms the task selected in their diagnostic and what output would be useful. |
| 15–40 | We perform that task live with an authorized case, fictitious if permission for real data is missing, in the role's assistant. The case owner reviews and decides whether it is useful. |
| 40–55 | Everyone repeats with their own case. We help. |
| 55–60 | Agree on a bounded practice and where to record outcome, minutes, corrections and support. |

Session rules: the person's process, with examples faithful to its structure and permitted data; the person keeps the judgment and the credit. Agree on any proposed separate panel with the client before enabling it.

## "Empieza aquí" (start here) page, one per role

The page itself is written in Spanish for the client. Its structure:

> **Start here: <role>**
>
> **What this is for:** <one line from the sponsor>.
>
> **Your assistant:** <link to the role's project or GPT>. It already knows <documents loaded>.
>
> **Next step:** <task selected in the diagnostic and ready-to-paste template>.
>
> **It is right if:** <controls the person checks before using the output>.
>
> **What it must not do:** <e.g. send anything to a customer without your review; receive card data or passwords>.
>
> **If something looks wrong:** write to <internal champion>.
>
> **Tell us how it went:** <authorized channel for outcome, minutes and support; no sensitive files or outputs in a separate panel>.

## Monthly follow-up

The team reports its experience. Propose a 30-minute monthly meeting, subject to agreement: what worked, what failed and which task is next. Claim savings only with a measured baseline and comparable scope.
